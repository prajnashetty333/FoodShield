# FOODSHIELD Final Commodity Concordance

## Wheat
**FBS Commodity:** Wheat and products

**Primary Trade Commodities:**
- Wheat (CPC: '0111)

**Core Trade Commodities (For Analysis):**
- Wheat

**Processed Commodities (Excluded):**
- Pastry
- Wheat and meslin flour
- Bread
- Food preparations of flour, meal or malt extract
- Flour of mustard seed
- Uncooked pasta, not stuffed or otherwise prepared
- Flour of cereals n.e.c.
- Flour of fruits
- Flour of cassava
- Flour of maize
- Flour of mixed grain
- Flour of pulses
- Flour of rice
- Flour, meal, powder, flakes, granules and pellets of potatoes
- Flour of roots and tubers n.e.c.
- Flour of rye
- Flour of sorghum
- Bulgur
- Barley flour and grits
- Flour of millet

**Excluded Byproducts/Feed/Non-Food:**
- Bran of wheat

**Uncertain Commodities:**
- Germ of wheat

**Methodological Reasoning:** Direct mapping to primary agricultural product to avoid double-counting processed layers.
